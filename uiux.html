What is UI/UX Design?

UI (User Interface) design focuses on how a website or digital product looks — the layout, colours, buttons, and visual elements. UX (User Experience) design focuses on how it feels to use — how easy it is to navigate, complete tasks, and achieve goals.

Strong UI/UX design makes websites clearer, more enjoyable, and more effective at turning visitors into customers.
