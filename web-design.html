What is Web Design?

Web design is the process of creating the visual look, layout, and user experience of a website. It focuses on how your site appears, how easy it is to navigate, and how effectively it communicates your brand to visitors.

A well-designed website builds trust, keeps visitors engaged, and guides them toward taking action — whether that’s making an enquiry, booking a service, or making a purchase.
