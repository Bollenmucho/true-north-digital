What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) is the process of improving your website so it ranks higher on search engines like Google. The goal is to increase the quantity and quality of traffic your site receives through organic search results.

When done well, SEO helps potential customers find your business when they are actively searching for the products or services you offer.
